<a id="overview"></a>

**Company of Heroes** is an exciting real-time military strategy game developed by THQ for Windows. Dive into the gripping action of World War II, specifically during the D-Day landings, and take control of your soldiers in this immersive experience. The **latest version** ensures you have access to all features and updates, providing a complete gameplay experience. 

With stunning graphics and realistic scenarios, **Company of Heroes** offers both a campaign mode and an engaging online multiplayer mode, allowing you to command Allied forces or even lead the German army.
